# Suggested Vimeo title

VITALINK — Offline Maternal Care & Referral Demo | Winny Darlen

## Suggested description

Presented by Winny Darlen for Health Hackathon 3.0.

VITALINK demonstrates native Android maternal assessment without connectivity, on-device deterministic safety rules, encrypted local assessment snapshots and a durable referral outbox. When connectivity returns, the same referral reaches the real Java/PostgreSQL backend and doctor workspace. An explicit clinician acknowledgment returns to Android with its actor and timestamp.

This video shows actual application footage with English synthesized narration and captions. The demonstration uses synthetic records. The footage is edited for presentation; it is not a performance benchmark.

Research prototype only: not a validated medical device. No diagnosis, medication advice, validated predictive score or clinical accuracy claim. Follow local escalation procedures immediately for urgent findings; do not wait for synchronization.
