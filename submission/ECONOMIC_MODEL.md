# Economic scenario

See ../docs/ECONOMIC_MODEL.md for the full assumption table and exclusions.

Illustrative equipment allocation INR10,000 over 24 months at 150 screenings/month plus INR5 consumables yields INR7.78 per screening. This is an arithmetic scenario, not measured cost or savings. It excludes staff, training, transport, maintenance, phone/connectivity, hosting, governance and clinical follow-up. No lives-saved or cost-effectiveness claim is made.

Pilot evaluation should measure handoff reliability, receipt/acknowledgment times and staff burden alongside actual total costs.
