# Economics and impact assumptions

No field cost study, clinical-outcome benefit, deployment budget or procurement quote has been established. This is a transparent planning scenario, not measured savings, ROI or a cost-effectiveness claim.

Let equipment allocation E = **INR 10,000**, useful life L = **24 months**, volume V = **150 screenings/month**, and incremental consumables C = **INR 5/screening**. These are illustrative assumptions chosen to explain the arithmetic, not market prices or measured operating costs.

`equipment_and_consumables_per_screening = E / (L × V) + C`

At the illustrative volume: `10,000 / (24 × 150) + 5 = INR 7.78/screening`.

| Monthly volume assumption | Equipment + consumables per screening |
|---|---:|
| 50 | INR 13.33 |
| 150 | INR 7.78 |
| 300 | INR 6.39 |

Excluded: staff time, training, travel/transport, BP-device calibration and replacement, maintenance, phone depreciation, data connectivity, hosting, clinical follow-up, privacy/security operations, governance, and opportunity costs. Server operation costs are not measured; no paid service was provisioned. Software being open source does not imply zero operational cost.

Future pilot measures should include referral handoff time, receipt and acknowledgment time separately, undelivered referrals, duplicate/conflicting records, staff documentation burden and transport bottlenecks. Use an approved protocol, baseline comparison, denominator definitions and confidence intervals. Acknowledgment is not a patient outcome. No lives-saved, mortality reduction or avoided-admission claim is justified by this hackathon.
